Mortal Kombat II - HIGHLIGHTED REVIEW

Rating: ★★★★★

  • I really enjoyed watching this movie. The fight scenes were awesome, especially the one with the black hole ore whatever that was meant to be in the background. Epic movie.

Metal Gear Solid

Rating: ★★★★★

  • Absolute peak game series. Literally revolutionized a entire game genre. And the fact that with the first game, it was a 2D game that was pretty much 3D.

  • Favorite character is Solid Snake, since he who you play as :P
